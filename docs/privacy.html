Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

QANAKAGON is a mental math practice app. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

QANAKAGON does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information. The app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking.

Scores and local storage

Challenge results, accuracy, and personal rankings are saved locally on your device so you can review your practice history. This information is not sent to us or shared with third parties. Deleting the app removes locally stored results.

Third-party services

The app does not send gameplay data to an analytics, advertising, or social networking service. Apple may process information according to its own policies when you download or use the app and its operating system.

Children’s privacy

Because QANAKAGON does not collect personal information, it is designed to minimize privacy risks for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit QANAKAGON support.
